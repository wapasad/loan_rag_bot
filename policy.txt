Personal Loan Policy - Test Document

1. Loan Amount: Customers can apply for a personal loan ranging from Tk 50,000 to Tk 1,000,000.

2. Interest Rate: The annual interest rate for personal loans is 9% to 12%, depending on the customer's credit score and income.

3. Tenure: Loan repayment tenure ranges from 12 months to 60 months.

4. Eligibility: Applicants must be between 21 and 60 years old, with a minimum monthly income of Tk 25,000.

5. Required Documents: National ID card, salary certificate, bank statement of last 6 months, and two passport-size photographs.

6. Processing Fee: A one-time processing fee of 1% of the loan amount applies.

7. Early Settlement: Customers may settle the loan early with a 2% early settlement fee on the remaining principal.
